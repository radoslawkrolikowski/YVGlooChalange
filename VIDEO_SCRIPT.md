# Round — video script

**"The Line She Stopped At"**
Runtime 2:50 · Format 16:9, phone screens shot in-hand · One character, one arc

---

## Before you shoot: two notes

**Marisol is a dramatization, not a testimonial.** She is a composite character
performed by an actor. Every screen she touches is the real product, but she is
not a real user and the film must never imply she is. Keep the on-screen line in
the end card — `Dramatization. Round is a real, working product; Marisol is not
a real user.` — and do not cut it. A fabricated testimonial presented as genuine
is the one thing that would sink an otherwise honest submission.

**Everything shown must be real.** No mockups of features that do not exist. The
flow below — Spanish RVES passage, pre-reading prompts, reflection, translated
circle message with "Show original", the overlap callout in the digest, the
prayer tab — is all live at https://yv-gloo-chalange.vercel.app. Shoot the
actual app. Judges will open it.

---

## The story in one line

A woman who moved countries and lost her language finds a circle of strangers
who understand what she said — in her own words.

---

## Script

### 0:00 – 0:22 — Cold open: the silence

| | |
|---|---|
| **VISUAL** | Manchester, November, 6:40am. Rain on a bus window. MARISOL (34) rests her head against the glass. Cut: an open-plan office, her saying "Yeah — no, that's fine, that works" three times to three different people, smile arriving a half-second late each time. Cut: her flat, coat still on, kettle boiling for nobody. |
| **VO (Marisol, Spanish, subtitled)** | *Aprendí inglés en seis meses.* (I learned English in six months.) <br><br> *Lo que nunca aprendí fue cómo rezar en él.* (What I never learned was how to pray in it.) |
| **SOUND** | Rain. Kettle rising. No music yet. |

> **Direction:** No sad-piano. The first 22 seconds should feel *competent*, not
> pitiful. Marisol is fine. That is the point — she is functioning, fluent,
> and completely alone in the language that holds her inner life.

---

### 0:22 – 0:40 — The Bible on the shelf

| | |
|---|---|
| **VISUAL** | Her Spanish Bible on a shelf, spine cracked, dust on the top edge. Her hand reaches for it — stops — takes the phone charging next to it instead. Beat. She scrolls. Nothing. |
| **VO** | *Lo abría. Leía tres versículos. Y luego...* (I'd open it. Read three verses. And then—) <br><br> *no tenía a nadie a quien decirle lo que había leído.* (I had no one to tell what I'd read.) |
| **SOUND** | First note of music enters — single sustained string, very low. |

---

### 0:40 – 1:05 — Instant Access, and the question nobody asks

| | |
|---|---|
| **VISUAL** | Real screen capture, shot in-hand. She taps **Instant Access**. No form. No account. Onboarding: *What do you want to learn?* She types slowly, in Spanish. Then the language picker — she selects **Español**, then **RVES**. |
| **VO** | *No me pidió una cuenta. Me preguntó qué quería aprender.* (It didn't ask me for an account. It asked what I wanted to learn.) |
| **ON SCREEN** | Hold 1.5s on the version chip: **RVES** |
| **SOUND** | Strings widen. Warmth entering. |

> **Direction:** This is the product's first promise and it must land as
> *relief*, not as UI. Shoot her face, not the screen, on "qué quería aprender."

---

### 1:05 – 1:28 — The passage, in her own language

| | |
|---|---|
| **VISUAL** | The **pre-reading prompts** card expands — two short prompts, generated for her. Then the passage: Salmo 1, Spanish, from YouVersion, RVES chip beside the reference. Her thumb drags across a line. The highlight blooms. |
| **VO** | *Y llegó en español. No traducido. En español.* (And it came in Spanish. Not translated. In Spanish.) |
| **ON SCREEN** | Highlighted line, held 2s |
| **SOUND** | Music opens fully for the first time. |

---

### 1:28 – 1:48 — The 2am reflection

| | |
|---|---|
| **VISUAL** | Timestamp: 2:14am. Dark room, phone light on her face. She types a reflection in Spanish — we see fragments, not the whole thing. Something honest. Something heavier than she'd say out loud. She hesitates over **Post**. Posts it. |
| **VISUAL (cont.)** | A quiet card appears — soft, small, no alarm, no red. Someone to talk to. A number. It is on *her* screen only. She looks at it for a long moment. Doesn't tap it. Puts the phone face-down. Breathes out. |
| **VO** | *Escribí algo que no le había dicho a nadie.* (I wrote something I hadn't told anyone.) <br><br> *Y no se lo dijo a nadie.* (And it told no one.) |
| **SOUND** | Music drops to near-silence under "no se lo dijo a nadie." |

> **Direction:** Restraint is everything here. She is not in crisis; she is
> carrying something heavy at 2am, which is most people. The card is quiet and
> private and she does not act on it — that is more honest and more moving than
> a rescue. Eight seconds. Do not extend it.
>
> **Product truth:** the Escalation Agent runs on every reflection before
> anything else touches it. Flagged content never reaches the circle. Only the
> reference is logged, never the words.

---

### 1:48 – 2:12 — The circle answers

| | |
|---|---|
| **VISUAL** | Morning. Bus again — but she's sitting up. She opens the circle thread. A reply from **TOM** (60s, Leeds). It is in English on his phone. On hers, it is in **Spanish**, with a small label: *Translated by Round.* She taps **Show original** — sees his English — taps back to Spanish. Small, involuntary smile. |
| **VO** | *Él escribió en inglés. Yo lo leí en español.* (He wrote in English. I read it in Spanish.) <br><br> *Y mis palabras — él las leyó en las suyas.* (And my words — he read them in his.) |
| **VISUAL (cont.)** | Quick cuts: the same thread on three phones in three languages. Same words. Different hands. |
| **SOUND** | Music lifts. First percussive element — a heartbeat pulse. |

---

### 2:12 – 2:32 — The line she stopped at

| | |
|---|---|
| **VISUAL** | The daily **digest** card. Camera pushes slowly in on one sentence — the overlap callout: *Marisol and Tom both stopped at the same line.* |
| **VISUAL (cont.)** | Cut to Tom, alone in a kitchen in Leeds, reading the same digest. He nods at nothing. |
| **VO** | *No sabía su cara. No sabía su edad.* (I didn't know his face. I didn't know his age.) <br><br> *Sabía dónde se había detenido.* (I knew where he stopped.) |
| **SOUND** | Full arrangement. This is the peak. |

> **Direction:** This is the emotional thesis of the entire film. Nobody was
> ranked. Nobody was compared. Two people were simply noticed landing on the
> same words. Give the digest sentence a full 3 seconds on screen.

| | |
|---|---|

| **VO** | *No me devolvió mi país.* (It didn't give me my country back.) <br><br> *Me devolvió mi idioma. Con gente dentro.* (It gave me my language back. With people in it.) |

## Production notes

**Casting.** Marisol must be able to carry 20 seconds with no dialogue. Cast for
stillness, not expressiveness. Tom appears for 4 seconds and should look like
someone's actual uncle, not an actor.

**Language.** Marisol speaks Spanish throughout with burned-in English subtitles.
Do not dub her. A film about someone losing their language cannot be in English.

**Screen capture.** Record the real app at
https://yv-gloo-chalange.vercel.app — screen-record clean at device resolution,
then composite into the in-hand phone shots. Do not film a screen directly;
moiré will destroy the passage text.

**Colour.** Cold blue-grey for 0:00–1:05. Warmth enters with the Spanish
passage at 1:05 and increases through the film. The last shot is the only one
with direct sunlight.

**Music.** Single sustained string → add cello at 0:40 → open at 1:05 → drop to
near-silence at 1:40 → heartbeat pulse at 1:48 → full at 2:12. No lyrics. No
worship-track cliché.

**What NOT to show.** No leaderboards, no streaks, no progress bars, no "Day 47
🔥". Round deliberately has none of these — showing one would contradict the
product's core design constraint.

---

## 60-second cutdown

For social. Keep: 0:00–0:22 (silence), 1:05–1:28 (passage in Spanish),
1:48–2:12 (the translated reply), 2:12–2:22 (overlap callout), end card.
Cut: the Bible on the shelf, onboarding, the 2am beat.

The 2am beat is the first thing to go in any cutdown. It needs the surrounding
quiet to land, and without it, it reads as a crisis ad.

## If this is the Kaggle submission video

This script is an **ad** — it sells the feeling, not the build. The competition
asks for a video, and judges also need to see that the thing works.

Two options:

1. **Ad + demo, one file.** Run this at 2:50, then hard-cut to 30–60 seconds of
   plain screen capture with no music: Instant Access → passage → reflection →
   digest → Agent Console. Total ~3:30. Exceeds the 3-minute limit.
2. **Ad only at 2:50, product proof in the writeup.** Safer for the limit; the
   writeup and public repo carry the technical evidence.

**Recommendation:** cut the ad to **2:20** using the cutdown logic above, then
append **40 seconds** of silent screen capture. Under 3:00, and a judge sees both
why it matters and that it runs.
